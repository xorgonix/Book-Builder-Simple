# Outline + Drafting Visual Guide

Use this when you need a screen-by-screen map from intake to polished chapter.

## Flow map

```
1. Intake panel
	|
	v
2. Book Brief
	|
	v
3. Outline & Chapter Shells
	|
	v
4. Chapter cockpit
	|
	+--> Step 1: Draft
	+--> Step 2: Diagnose
	+--> Step 3: Revise
	+--> Step 4: Polish & Lock

Fast path:
Full Autopilot --> Chapter cockpit
```

## Screen cards

### 1. Intake panel

Where it is:
- Left column.

What you enter:
- Book type.
- Target length.
- Target chapter count.
- Core topic or premise.
- Target audience.
- Reader hunger or emotional pull.
- Prohibited directions.
- Author intent.
- Selected tone.

What you click:
- Save Intake.

What you should see:
- The step bar advances.
- The app is ready for Book Brief generation.

If you get stuck:
- Use Inspire Me or Surprise Me on the reader hunger field.

### 2. Book Brief

Where it appears:
- Middle workspace after Generate Book Brief.

What it is:
- The first reviewable plan for the book.
- The bridge between intake and outline.

What to check:
- Title.
- Subtitle.
- Promise.
- Voice tone.
- What it is.
- What it is not.

What you click next:
- Generate Outline & Chapter Shells, only after the brief looks right.

### 3. Outline & Chapter Shells

Where it appears:
- Middle workspace after the brief exists.

What it is:
- The working table of contents.
- Each chapter is a shell with a title, purpose, and status.

What to check:
- Chapter order.
- Chapter title.
- Chapter purpose.
- Chapter status.
- The brief summary still visible above the list.

What you click next:
- Open a chapter cockpit to draft one chapter.

Current limitation:
- Chapter cards are reviewable, but they do not yet have full approve/edit/regenerate controls.

### 4. Chapter cockpit

Where it appears:
- Middle workspace when one chapter is open.

What you see:
- A four-tab chapter workspace.

The tabs:
- Raw Draft Copy.
- Expert Editorial Diagnosis.
- Targeted Revision.
- Polished Manuscript.

What each tab means:
- Raw Draft Copy: first draft output.
- Expert Editorial Diagnosis: structural critique.
- Targeted Revision: rewrite based on the diagnosis.
- Polished Manuscript: final cleaned chapter.

What you click:
- Step 1: Draft.
- Step 2: Diagnose.
- Step 3: Revise.
- Step 4: Polish & Lock.

What to do if the diagnosis needs your input:
- Add manual correction notes in the diagnosis area before revising.

## Fast path vs manual path

Use Full Autopilot when:
- You want the app to run without stopping for review.
- You are testing end-to-end generation.

Use the manual cockpit when:
- You want to inspect each stage.
- You want to correct structure before the next pass.
- You want to understand what the system is doing.

## Recovery path

If you feel lost, do this:

1. Check intake first.
2. Read the Book Brief second.
3. Review the outline third.
4. Open one chapter cockpit and run the four steps in order.
